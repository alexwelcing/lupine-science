# Data licence

Unless a file says otherwise, first-party data shipped in this repository is
licensed under:

Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0

See: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

"First-party data" means the structured records Lupine Science produced and
publishes here: for example the JSON and CSV files under `public/data/`, the
evidence-record manifests (`*.proofpack.json`) and the hash manifests that
accompany them.

Third-party data keeps its own terms. In particular:

- `public/data/mof5_structure.json` is derived from Crystallography Open
  Database entry 1516287 (public domain); the structure was first reported in
  Li, Eddaoudi, O'Keeffe & Yaghi, Nature 402, 276 (1999).
- Reference values drawn from published papers and databases are credited to
  their sources in the files and articles that use them.

Released, hash-locked packs keep their original text so their checksums stay
valid. The Savings Stack v1 pack (`public/data/savings-stack-v1/`, released
2026-07-21) states ODbL-1.0 in its own README; since 2026-11-11 its
first-party data is also licensed under CC BY 4.0, as recorded in `NOTICE.md`.
