# Frame direction

A dark institutional scientific-film system built from material cross-sections, force vectors, fields, and measured boundaries. Color is materially present, not restricted to tiny labels: the correction layer is oxidized copper, water-bearing pathways are cool blue, air-bearing pathways are muted lichen, and raw bias or unsafe state is evidence red. These colors may occupy mechanism surfaces and localized atmospheric fields but never encode unsupported magnitude or probability.

## Semantic palette

- Mineral black is the persistent institutional field.
- Structural cream is neutral evidence and settled output.
- Oxidized copper marks measured correction, transfer, or verified mechanism.
- Water blue marks water-bearing pathways and the moving measured-domain point.
- Lichen marks air-bearing pathways and the supported claim state.
- Evidence red marks raw bias, synthesis-dependent state, or warning.
- Color is never decorative and never introduces a scientific category absent from the contract.

## Composition

- One dominant proof per scene.
- At most three required visible relationships per scene.
- Full-frame diagrams, not equal card grids.
- Large labels only when they identify a mechanism or state.
- Persistent origin/reference marks make changes legible across time.
- Every settled scene contains a clearly visible branded color field or mechanism surface, not only a low-opacity glow or small label.
- No decorative molecule wallpaper, holograms, generic futuristic UI, gradients in text, or paper-and-indigo treatment.

## Typography

- Headlines: Newsreader, 72–104 px, 650–800 weight equivalent.
- Explanatory labels: IBM Plex Sans, 28–40 px.
- State and source labels: IBM Plex Mono, 20–26 px.
- No body copy below 24 px in rendered video.

## Motion

- Deterministic and seek-safe.
- Motion always identifies cause, transformation, comparison, or domain state.
- Cuts preserve at least one persistent reference element when the causal object continues.
- No perpetual loops, random particles, decorative parallax, or motion that obscures labels.

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA text contrast.
- Narration is fully captioned in English WebVTT.
- No autoplay or looping in article placement.
- Poster and transcript remain complete reduced-motion alternatives.
