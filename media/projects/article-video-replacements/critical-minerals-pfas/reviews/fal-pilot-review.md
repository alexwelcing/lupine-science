## Shared defect class and correction

The model cannot be trusted to author multi-path process topology from prose. Subsequent Fal generation is restricted to architectural/material atmosphere plates with no process equipment, piping, controls, text, or measurements. Exact pipes, cartridges, vessels, barriers, states, labels, and flow relationships will be authored deterministically and composited over those plates. Fal remains the primary environmental visual source; deterministic overlays own all scientific and mechanical logic.
