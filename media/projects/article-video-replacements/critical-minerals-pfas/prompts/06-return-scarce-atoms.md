# 06 — Return scarce atoms

Classification: Speculative worldbuilding artwork informed by materials culture. Not a forecast, engineering design, scientific reference, simulation result, or evidence of a real deployment.

Render a wide 16:9 cutaway-oblique view of a maintainable critical-mineral recovery pilot inside a practical public-works bay. A complex brine feed enters one removable ion-selective cartridge cassette. The selected fraction exits through a physically distinct narrow line into one modest recovered-mineral vessel. A parallel wash loop removes the cassette and returns it to service after regeneration. Make cartridge access, rails, lifting points, seals, drain pans, hose routing, pump supports, corroded fasteners, mineral residue, wet floor edges, and service clearances mechanically coherent. No magical transparent pipes; only small truthful sight-glass sections. No claims of yield, purity, scale, or validated performance. Palette: mineral black structure, structural cream housings, oxidized copper transfer path, water blue feed, lichen-air regeneration loop. Quiet ordinary light, no dramatic clouds, no glossy stock laboratory, no molecule hero, no generated words, numbers, gauges, logos, pseudo-data, or people as subjects.
