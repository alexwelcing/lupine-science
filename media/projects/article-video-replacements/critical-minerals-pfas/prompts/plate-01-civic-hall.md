# Plate 01 — civic water hall, no process machinery

Classification: Speculative worldbuilding artwork informed by materials culture. Not a forecast, architectural proposal, engineering design, scientific reference, simulation result, or evidence of a real deployment.

Render a wide 16:9 empty Midwestern municipal public-works hall as a cinematic architectural and material atmosphere plate. Long symmetrical interior with a broad central service aisle, two clearly separated recessed process bays—one left and one right—and a narrow raised central service bridge that could support later compositing. Include only architecture and passive infrastructure: mineral-black steel columns and roof trusses, structural-cream glazed block and washable wall panels, oxidized-copper wear on empty support brackets, pale water-blue floor drainage channels, lichen-toned removable wall panels, concrete plinths, floor drains, handrails, overhead maintenance crane rails, access ladders, bolt holes, pipe-support brackets with no pipes installed, condensation marks, mineral residue, mop wear, ordinary public-institution daylight through clerestory windows. Preserve generous empty zones in both bays for deterministic equipment overlays. No tanks, pipes, pumps, columns, cartridges, vessels, treatment machines, control panels, screens, gauges, labels, signs, lettering, numbers, logos, molecular imagery, people, dramatic sky, smoke, steam spectacle, neon, cyberpunk, glossy sci-fi, or luxury architecture. Camera at human eye level, centered but not sterile, credible maintenance access. Dominant purpose: an ownable civic/material world in which exact process topology can later be composited.
