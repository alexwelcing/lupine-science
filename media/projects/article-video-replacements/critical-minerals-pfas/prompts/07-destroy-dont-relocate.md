# 07 — Destroy, don’t relocate

Classification: Speculative worldbuilding artwork informed by materials culture. This depicts a proposed contained pilot pathway, not a commercial catalyst, validated destruction process, forecast, engineering design, simulation result, or evidence of deployment.

Render a wide 16:9 contained PFAS treatment pilot in a municipal remediation bay. Contaminated water first passes through a packed sorbent column with visible maintenance access. The captured contaminant is transferred through a sealed, double-contained line into a compact destructive-treatment pilot cell behind a protective service enclosure. A separate clean effluent sampling line leaves the capture stage. Make the contrast physical: capture concentrates the burden; sealed transfer prevents release; the destruction cell is clearly a cautious pilot, not a proven commercial machine. Show secondary containment, drip trays, gasketed flanges, ventilation ducting, sample ports without labels, removable media canisters, wash-down surfaces, access doors, lockout hardware, residue and ordinary wear. No smoke, flames, glowing reaction chamber, visible emissions, landfill imagery, heroic success, or impossible glass reactors. Palette: mineral black structure, structural cream enclosure, water blue influent/effluent glimpses, oxidized copper sealed transfer path, restrained evidence red only on containment geometry. No generated text, symbols, labels, gauges, screens, numbers, molecular imagery, portraits, neon, cyberpunk, or dramatic weather. One dominant proof: contaminants move from capture into a sealed proposed destruction pathway rather than being visibly discarded.
