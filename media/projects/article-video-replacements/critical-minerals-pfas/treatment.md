# Treatment — Recover what we need. Destroy what we don’t.

## Hook

A single public-works hall receives two waterborne burdens from opposite sides: mineral-rich brine and PFAS-contaminated water. The streams never mingle. They pass through parallel, serviceable cartridge lines whose outputs move in opposite moral directions—one vessel receives recovered ions; one sealed reactor receives concentrated contaminants. The first read is physical, not textual.

## Arc

1. **Opposite crises, same atomic problem.** Establish scarce atoms versus persistent molecules as two civic flows.
2. **Selectivity is the machine.** Show that both outcomes depend on what a pore or active site binds, releases, or breaks.
3. **The difficult bond.** Render C–F activation and ion selectivity as exact deterministic relationships, not molecular spectacle.
4. **Why fast models lie here.** Show raw-model softening at pores, surfaces, vacancies, and transition states.
5. **Measure, correct, bound.** Introduce the environment error field and its supported domain.
6. **Return scarce atoms.** Follow a maintainable recovery loop from complex feed to regenerated cartridge and recovered fraction.
7. **Stop relocating PFAS.** Follow capture into a sealed destructive-treatment pilot with explicit containment; do not imply commercial success.
8. **One shared test discipline.** Both cartridge classes enter the same controlled flow-and-regeneration fixture.
9. **Close the loop honestly.** Supported candidates advance; unsupported environments stop at the boundary. Recover what we need. Destroy what we don’t.

## Visual method

### Fal-authored scenes

Fal renders public-works architectural atmosphere plates, material wear, drainage, access paths, ordinary light, and human-scale consequences. After a three-shot pilot failed topology review, Fal is prohibited from owning process equipment, pipes, cartridges, vessels, controls, measurements, or flow logic. These plates are classified as speculative worldbuilding artwork informed by materials culture—not engineering designs, forecasts, deployments, simulations, or scientific evidence. No generated text, gauges, logos, equations, or molecular structures.

### Deterministic scenes

Code graphics and structured compositing render:

- all process equipment, pipes, cartridges, vessels, containment, and maintenance topology;
- four-ion pore selectivity;
- C–F activation versus incomplete capture/relocation;
- raw-versus-corrected barrier ranking;
- measured-domain boundary and abstention;
- final supported/unsupported decision state.

Each scene carries one dominant proof and at most three visible relationships. Labels, geometry, and motion carry meaning without relying on color.

## Motion language

- Flow follows pipes, channels, cartridges, and transfer arms; no decorative particles.
- Camera moves only to reveal topology, maintenance access, or before/after material state.
- Exact graphics use measured trajectories, ranked lanes, and bounded regions.
- Scene transitions follow matter: feed → pocket → barrier → correction → test → disposition.
- No generic dissolves between unrelated stills.

## Audio

Retain the recovered 322-word narration if decoded-audio verification reproduces it and no prohibited claim is present. Keep narration dry and intelligible; use low, restrained industrial room tone only if it materially improves continuity. No heroic trailer music.

## Authored review anchors

- **Early:** shared hall with two incoming streams and physically distinct output paths.
- **Proof:** raw/corrected ranking at the under-coordinated boundary.
- **Late:** common test fixture plus supported/unsupported disposition before the closing line.

## Rejection rules

Reject any candidate that:

- makes the recovery and remediation lines visually interchangeable;
- implies a commercial PFAS destruction catalyst exists;
- shows unlabeled generated measurements or pseudo-data;
- uses molecule-as-hero imagery instead of physical systems and exact overlays;
- obscures the dominant proof with atmospheric spectacle;
- encodes epistemic status only by color;
- introduces unsupported market, impact, probability, or performance claims;
- lets generated frames depict deterministic evidence;
- falls back to paper-and-indigo or generic cinematic laboratory imagery.
