## Visual thesis

Two opposite material flows share one hidden geometry:

- scarce atoms are recovered and returned to circulation;
- persistent molecules are captured and destroyed;
- both depend on selective binding and barrier energies in under-coordinated environments;
- the correction layer determines which rankings are supportable.
