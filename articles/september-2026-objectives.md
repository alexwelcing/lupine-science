## What shipped in August

- **Shared-anchor proof pack published.** Our locked analysis of 29 analyzable diffusion paths shows that sharing identical reference calculations across four model guides reduces the projected evaluation count from 558 to 154 — 72.4% fewer — published as a web article, a downloadable PDF, and a machine-readable manifest. [Read the proof pack →](/articles/shared-dft-anchors/)
- **Environment-error-field paper published.** A smooth, environment-resolved error field for machine-learned interatomic potentials, published as a public proof pack with locked figures. [Read the article →](/articles/a-smooth-environment-resolved-error-field/)
- **Sharp correction licenses accepted.** Six new machine-checked theorems replaced conservative worst-case caps with exact calibration-only correction licenses. The public theorem inventory now stands at {{LEAN_THEOREM_COUNT}} machine-counted declarations with zero unfinished proofs.
- **A registered correction trial completed — and reported honestly.** The Round-4 trial returned a negative result in both registered material classes. We published the failure rather than smoothing it over, because the point of preregistration is that the answer counts even when we dislike it. [What the cap was hiding →](/articles/what-the-cap-was-hiding/)
- **Five-part climate series completed.** All five articles on materials that matter for climate — from cement to refrigerants — are live. [Browse the series →](/articles/)
- **Literature corpus fully audited.** Every citation identifier across our nine literature digests was verified: 115 of 115 arXiv identifiers and 48 of 48 DOIs resolve to the intended sources.
- **Publication asset library certified.** One hundred article-mapped images and five short films passed independent certification, closing the quality gate on our visual publication program.
- **Article media remediation shipped.** A large publication-quality backlog across article audio and video was cleared, and the packaging pipeline now fails closed on missing provenance instead of shipping quietly.

## Currently in flight

- **An upgraded correction estimator.** The science behind our optimal-bias estimator has been accepted by independent review; integration into the main research codebase is underway and awaits green CI and a fresh review before it can land.
- **A configuration-space bridge test.** The corrected record for this cross-dataset model test returned an inconclusive verdict — the mandatory data gate could not be satisfied, which we treat as an information refusal rather than a result. Independent review of the corrected record is pending.
- **A preregistered Round-5 trial.** The design — 125 elastic structures evaluated across four registered models — is registered and locked. Execution waits on fail-closed infrastructure gates, not on enthusiasm.

## How to read this page

We hold public work to the same gates as internal work: hypotheses are preregistered before execution, results are reviewed independently before integration, missing evidence fails closed rather than being filled in, and negative results are published alongside positive ones. Where an objective above slips, next month's page will say so.
