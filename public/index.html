Before a lab spends a year on a material, know whether to trust the prediction.

Lupine Science measures where machine-learned models of atoms go wrong, against published references and pass/fail gates set in advance, and publishes every verdict, failures included.

Our mission: Make AI predictions of new materials checkable before a lab commits to them.

Supported · narrow scope

A blind prediction held

For nine fcc metals, an error field fitted to three other properties predicted the never-fitted (110) surface-energy error of four foundation models, significantly for three of the four.

Open · not deployable

A correction ceiling, not a fix

On 16 cubic metals, an elastic-constant correction helped only when the held-out answer set how far to correct. One that works without seeing the answer is still open.

The 16-metal result →

Failed · three failed, one abstained

Round 4: no gate passed

In July 2026, migration barriers, a lattice and elastic correction, and a catalyst correction failed. Magnetism abstained: no available model had the spin physics it needed.
