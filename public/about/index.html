How it works

Measure
Prediction errors of interatomic potentials are not random. In the committed benchmark they collapse onto a low-dimensional ribbon, and the dominant error directions are largely shared across potentials.
Correct
A measured error field runs beside an existing MLIP as an additive energy term with analytic forces. No fine-tuning, no retraining.
Prove
Quantitative claims are sealed as Lean 4 theorems over provenance-hashed data. Corrections are licensed only where evidence supports them; unsupported cases abstain.

Questions investors and scientists ask

What does Lupine Science do?

Lupine Science is an early-stage deep-tech company building a verification layer for AI-driven materials discovery. It measures the structured error of machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs), corrects that error at runtime as a physical field beside the model, and seals its quantitative claims as machine-checked Lean 4 theorems over provenance-hashed data, in public.

What problem does it solve?

Generative models and machine-learned potentials now propose and screen far more candidate materials than any lab can synthesize. The bottleneck has moved from generating candidates to knowing which predictions to trust. A prediction checked before the bench saves the year a lab would otherwise spend finding out.

How is this different from building another interatomic potential?

Lupine Science does not compete with model builders. It sits between model generation and laboratory validation: model builders get external verification, and laboratories get shortlists with characterized failure modes. The correction runs beside an existing model, with no fine-tuning or retraining.

Is the work peer-reviewed?

Not yet. Every article carries its own status label (draft, final, live evidence), and every quantitative claim links to the committed data and the machine-checked Lean theorem it rests on, including the corrections that failed.

What stage is the company at, and is it raising?

Early stage, founder-led. Independent. No outside capital raised to date. Talking with deep-tech investors, materials labs, and MLIP builders ahead of TMS2027.
