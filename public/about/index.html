About Lupine Science

Lupine Science is an early-stage, founder-led research company. We independently measure the errors of machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs), test corrections against preregistered gates, record any amendment made after results are seen, and publish the verdict: pass, fail or abstain. Correction is a research question here, not a product: it has held only in narrow, stated cases. Nothing we have published is peer-reviewed yet.

How we work

  • Gates first: we write the pass/fail gate before a test runs, label any amendment made after results are seen, and publish the data, the gate and the verdict together: pass, fail or abstain.
  • Founder-led, with AI coding and research agents working under rules that live in code. AI assistance is disclosed, and a human author is responsible for every public summary.
  • Key-person risk is real: the program depends on one founder. Public data, code and the failure ledger mean the results can be checked without us.

Common questions

Is the work peer-reviewed?

No Lupine paper has been peer-reviewed or accepted yet. The TMS2027 poster is a conference presentation, not a paper. Each article carries a status label and links the data it uses.

Will newer multi-head models, with their own uncertainty estimates, make this unnecessary?

They may change what needs testing, and we have not tested them yet. A model’s own uncertainty still needs an outside, preregistered check against published references: models trained on similar data can agree and still be wrong. If you build one, we would like to test it.

Can I reuse your data?

Yes. You can reuse our code (MIT) and our first-party text and data (CC BY 4.0) with attribution and no share-alike obligations; third-party material keeps its own terms. The data is in lupine-rhizo.
