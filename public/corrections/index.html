Corrections log · updated November 12, 2026

Corrections

When we retire a public claim, we list it here with the date and the reason; what we claim now is on the results page, and older wording stays in our public git history.

“Verified AI predictions”

Was
“Verified AI predictions for materials discovery”; “the trust layer” as what we offer.
Now
We measure where machine-learned models of atoms go wrong and publish every verdict, failures included.
Why
Our evidence supports measurement, not verification. In Round 4 (July 2026) no gate passed.
Retired
November 11, 2026

Runtime correction as a product

Was
“corrects that error at runtime as a physical field beside the model”.
Now
Correction is a research question here, not a product: it has held only in narrow, stated cases.
Why
The one correction that held blind covers one model, two metals, one facet; both Round-4 correction tests failed.
Retired
November 11, 2026

“~70% of dominant error directions are shared”

Was
“~70% of dominant error directions are shared”; “errors span only 1.1–2.0 directions”.
Now
Retired as evidence. In our machine-learned benchmark, apparent cross-model alignment sat inside a coupling-aware null.
Why
It was a mean cosine over four classical (EAM) potentials, shown as if it described machine-learned ones.
Retired
November 11, 2026

Theorem counts as evidence

Was
“899 build-locked theorems in the open Lean library, zero sorry” as a headline.
Now
Lean 4 checks the arithmetic and stated inequalities behind published numbers; it does not check the physics, the reference data or the pipeline.
Why
A count of checked statements says nothing about whether the physics is right.
Retired
November 11, 2026

“17.84 → 9.92 GPa” and a usable class-aware correction

Was
Elastic error “17.84 → 9.92 GPa” and “zero no-harm violations”, presented as a usable correction.
Now
A ceiling, not a deployable correction: it needs the held-out answer to set its size. See results.
Why
9.92 GPa was an in-sample recalibration. Neither figure is a correction you could apply to a new material.
Retired
November 11, 2026

“5–12 GtCO₂/yr” as a Lupine outcome

Was
“Five Materials for 5–12 GtCO₂/Year”, as a climate outcome our work would unlock.
Now
Climate materials are one lane of where the work is aimed (Direction). Literature estimates appear only attributed to their sources.
Why
The range summed literature estimates. We have not measured or enabled any climate outcome.
Retired
November 11, 2026

Organisations described as partners

Was
Labs, companies and agencies named as partners or pilots, with partner tiers and maps.
Now
We cite published work as sources, and welcome labs that want their models or materials measured.
Why
None of the organisations named were partners.
Retired
November 11, 2026

Environmental-series capability claims

Was
One correction layer “fixes” water, air, PFAS, cement and refrigerant discovery; “corrected barriers recover DFT accuracy”; “certificates of synthesizability”.
Now
These pieces are marked Direction; each capability is a hypothesis to test.
Why
Our results contradict them: in Round 4 the barrier gate failed for every model, and both correction tests failed.
Retired
November 11, 2026

“Proof pack”

Was
“Proof pack” for our evidence bundles, including a climate-series pack.
Now
“Evidence record”. The climate-series pack is retired; the shared DFT anchors record was reissued on November 12, 2026, reviewed internally (AI-assisted).
Why
The bundles check file integrity and provenance; they do not prove the claims inside them.
Retired
November 11, 2026

The TMS2027 abstract’s universal “hyper-ribbon”

Was
The accepted poster abstract described a universal error “hyper-ribbon” that makes transferability predictable.
Now
A TMS2027 poster (Orlando, 14–18 March 2027), not a paper; the proceedings manuscript replaces it with narrower measurement claims.
Why
Registered stress tests did not support it, and a global elastic correction made results worse.
Retired
November 11, 2026

72.4% shown without its baseline

Was
“72.4% fewer DFT evaluations” with no baseline; booklet v1 called it “measured” and added ~38–76× multipliers.
Now
Projected, no new DFT, against four separate runs: only on the shared DFT anchors page and booklet v1.1.
Why
Without its baseline a projection read as a saving. The undemonstrated multipliers were removed on November 12, 2026.
Retired
November 11, 2026

“All four models under-predict every barrier”

Was
Round-4 and Z1 pages: every model under-predicted every barrier, 26 of 26 paths for one.
Now
Model-dependent at float64: CHGNet 25 of 28, MACE-MP-0 medium 21 of 29, small 17 of 26, MACE-MPA-0 medium 13 of 28 paths.
Why
That came from the float32 run; the locked float64 records show no universal direction.
Retired
November 11, 2026
